The Challenge

You may have tried to change before.

  • To stop overthinking.
  • To move on.

When you begin to understand those deeper layers, change becomes clearer, more intentional, and more sustainable.

Manila Founder · EnerSoul Healing

Hello, I'm

Manila

Counselling Psychologist  ·  Reiki Grand Master  ·  Holistic Healing Practitioner

I'm the founder of EnerSoul Healing, where psychology and holistic healing come together to support deeper self-understanding and transformation.

For almost eight years, I built my career in the corporate world — learning about people, behaviour and communication. From the outside, I was building a successful path. But somewhere along the way, I realised that another path was calling me.

My own experiences with emotional pain, disconnection and healing became a turning point. They led me inward — to understand myself, to explore the deeper layers of healing, and eventually to recognise what I truly wanted to dedicate my life to.

"I chose to leave behind the path I had known and follow a purpose that felt greater than a career."

Today, through psychological understanding and holistic practices including Reiki, meditation, hypnotherapy, tarot and crystal therapy, I create a space where healing can be explored in a way that is compassionate, intuitive, grounded and deeply personal.

I understand what it feels like to search for answers, and to slowly find your way back. And perhaps most importantly — choosing yourself takes courage.
