My Story

A path that chose me.

Before EnerSoul Healing, there was a woman searching—for answers, for purpose, for a life that felt truly her own.

The Beginning

Eight years building a familiar path.

For almost eight years, I built my career in the corporate world — learning about people, behaviour, communication and the many ways we navigate life.

From the outside, I was building a familiar and successful path. I had the career, the trajectory, the milestones that were supposed to mean something.

But somewhere along the way, I began to realise that there was another path calling me — one that felt more deeply connected to who I truly was.

8

Years in the corporate world

Learning deeply about people, behaviour and the many ways we navigate life — before finding a different calling.

The Turning Point

When the path turned inward.

My own experiences with emotional pain, disconnection and healing became a turning point. They led me inward — to understand myself, to explore the deeper layers of healing.

And eventually to recognise what I truly wanted to dedicate my life to.

That turning point wasn't comfortable. But it was honest. And it was mine.

"I chose to leave behind the path I had known and follow a purpose that felt greater than a career."

— Manila
The Foundation

Where two worlds became one.

My background in psychology gave me a deep understanding of the mind, human behaviour and emotional patterns. My own healing journey opened me to the deeper dimensions of inner work — exploring the relationship between the mind, body, energy and inner self.

Today, I bring these worlds together. Through psychological understanding and holistic practices including Reiki, meditation, hypnotherapy, tarot and crystal therapy, I create a space where healing can be explored in a way that is compassionate, intuitive and deeply personal.

What I Understand

Choosing yourself takes courage.

I understand what it feels like to know that something within you needs to change, yet not fully understand what it is. I understand what it means to lose connection with yourself, to search for answers, and to slowly find your way back.

And perhaps most importantly — I understand that choosing yourself and choosing a different life takes courage.

That is the space I bring into my work. A space where you can slow down, explore what lies beneath your experiences, and reconnect with the part of you that may have been waiting to be heard.

"I believe every person carries the capacity for healing and transformation. Sometimes, what we need is the right space, the right support, and someone who can truly understand the journey."

— Manila

EnerSoul Healing was born from my decision to follow that journey myself.
Today, I dedicate my life to helping others find their way towards greater understanding, inner connection and meaningful change.

If something within you is asking to be understood — I'd be honoured to walk alongside you.
